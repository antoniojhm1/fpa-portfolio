The brief

Why "Make the Turn"

The turn is the stop between the 9th and 10th holes: snacks, drinks, a little trash talk. It is the most social ten minutes in golf, and it is exactly what this brand is named after.

Audience

  • Social golfers, 22–40, who play scrambles, leagues and Topgolf nights
  • Golf-curious friends of golfers who buy for the look
  • Gift buyers in November–December

Single message

Every round needs a social. Caddie Social is the hat your group wears to it.

Proof points: perforated performance crown, rope-front classic shape, embroidered 3D logo.

Tone

  • Clubhouse, not country club
  • Warm, quick, a little cheeky
  • Group shots over solo hero shots
  • Golden hour, real grass, real laughs

Production order

Running it in Buzzy

  1. Upload assets/hat-front.jpg and assets/hat-three-quarter.jpg as product references in a new Buzzy project.
  2. Generate the Nano Pro product shots first. Approve one clean hat render before making any scenes.
  3. Feed the approved product shot into each GPT Image 2 scene prompt, with the product lock appended.
  4. Use the approved scene stills as start frames for Seedance 2.5. Generate each shot separately, then stitch.
  5. Check every frame for logo drift. Re-run any shot where "Caddie" or "SOCIAL" is misspelled or warped.
  6. Export 9:16 first, then 1:1 and 16:9 crops. Add captions; most viewers watch with sound off.
